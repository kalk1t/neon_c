# N06 — Canal wall module

## Scope

Individual transparent PNG modelling reference for **R1 — Neon Downpour**. This image is concept/reference art, not a mesh, UV atlas, measured elevation, PBR texture set or finished game object. Its appearance is based on the selected R1 map concept.

## Design targets

Target width × depth × height: 18 × 4 × 12 m. These are **design targets, not measurements recovered from the image**. Use Blender dimensions to establish exact scale; do not infer a guaranteed physical clearance from the raster.

- **Construction route:** Blender preferred: repeatable rectangular wall with small bevel and separately placed ledge. Use textured concrete detail instead of dense geometry.
- **Origin / pivot:** Midpoint of wall bottom at Z=0; local X along the 18 m run, local Y thickness, Z up. Ends at X=±9 m.
- **Collision:** One box body; optional ledge box. Keep end faces aligned and avoid collision gaps when duplicated.
- **LOD triangle targets:** LOD0 ≤ 4,500; LOD1 ≤ 1,500; LOD2 ≤ 160. Targets apply to the whole visible asset before instancing; adjust after profiling. These are budgets for future meshes, not measured counts.
- **Materials:** keep black/graphite body and glass separate from narrow emissive cyan/magenta inlays. Use shared materials/atlases where practical. Wetness belongs in roughness/normal treatment and lighting; avoid baking the map's rain and fog into the object texture.
- **Blender orientation:** values above use Z up. Check the exported glTF's up/forward convention against the game's existing importer rather than rotating it twice.
