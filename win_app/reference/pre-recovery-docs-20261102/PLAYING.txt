ROADSTERS RACE - FIRST FLIGHT

2026-11-02: A previous build caused a reported whole-PC freeze. The revised
renderer has compiled and passed headless checks only; graphics stability
and performance are unverified. No rerun is requested.

This is the first native C flight prototype, not the complete browser game.
Launch RoadstersRace.exe, then click START FLIGHT or press Enter.

W thrust              S brake / reverse
Mouse aim             A/D or left/right arrows steer
E/Q or up/down arrows climb / dive
Shift boost           F spin dodge
R reset to last gate  C switch near/far chase camera
Hold right mouse to look around
Esc or P pause/resume F11 fullscreen

An Xbox-layout gamepad is wired for left-stick steering/climb, RT thrust,
LT brake, A boost, B spin, Y camera, View reset and Menu pause/start.
Physical controller behavior has not yet been verified.

Fly the green guidance dots through the highlighted checkpoints in order.
This prototype counts practice checkpoints. It does not yet validate a full
race, award coins, or save progression.

Included: original Roadster model, fallback car, Skyline Loop route, a
temporary procedural city, checkpoint practice, HUD/minimap and pause.
Still to port: complete city/art/lighting, world collision response, rivals,
combat, audio, race results/progression, settings saves, accounts and online.

Startup and pause now use a 2D screen. The city/model load only after START
FLIGHT. Default rendering is capped at 960x600 internally, 30 FPS, no MSAA,
and glow off. --glow opts into glow at the bounded internal resolution.
Flight pauses after ten consecutive frames taking over 150 ms. This cannot
interrupt a blocked GPU/driver call or guarantee protection against a freeze.
Native diagnostics append to logs/roadsters.log beside the executable.

This x64 development build uses the Microsoft Visual C runtime (/MD).
The revised graphics have not been run or tested on a clean installation.
Keep the assets and licenses folders alongside the executable.
