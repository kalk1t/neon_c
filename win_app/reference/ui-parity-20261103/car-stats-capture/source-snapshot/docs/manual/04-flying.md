## Thrust, speed and brake
- Hold **W** (phone: automatic; gamepad: RT) to accelerate. Top speed is about **486 km/h** (302 mph; 342 km/h before 0.2.15).
- Let go and the car coasts, slowing gently.
- **S** brakes hard. Keep holding it at a standstill to fly backwards slowly (up to about 65 km/h).
- The speed panel (bottom left) shows **km/h**, or **mph** if you choose it in [Settings](08-settings.md).

## Steering
**With a mouse**, you don't steer the car directly: you move the **aim ring**, which stays in the middle of the screen
while the view turns with it, and the car turns and climbs toward it. The further the car's nose is from the ring, the
harder it turns. A small **dot** shows where the nose points while the car catches up. Your shots go where the ring
is. Flying backwards (hold **S**), the nose still turns toward the ring.

- The car turns faster at low speed than at top speed.
- Pressing **brake while turning** tightens the turn and lets the car drift.
- The nose tilts up or down at most about 35°. Climbing and diving follow the aim ring's height.
- Without a mouse the view follows the car and the big **crosshair** ring shows where it is heading.
- Keys (arrows, Q/E) and the gamepad stick steer directly instead.
- **A / D slide** the car sideways (up to about 90 km/h, at any speed) without turning it: dodge fire or line up a
  gate while your aim stays put. Without a mouse (keyboard only), A / D steer like the arrows.

## Boost
Hold **Shift** (phone: **BOOST**, gamepad: **A**) while thrusting.

- Top speed rises to about **706 km/h** (439 mph) and the car accelerates twice as hard.
- The screen reacts: a **NITRO** call-out, a flash at the edges, speed lines and a wider view.
- Boost uses the **boost bar** (bottom left). A full bar lasts about **3 seconds** of boosting.
- The bar refills slowly by itself (about 17 seconds from empty to full) and faster from play:
  **+30%** for every checkpoint ring and **+50%** for every takedown.
- You can't boost while stunned by a hit. The bar blinks when it is nearly empty.

## Cameras
Press **C** (gamepad: **Y**) to switch:

| Camera | View |
|---|---|
| **Near chase** (default) | Close behind the car |
| **Far chase** | Further back and higher: better for seeing what's around you |
| **First person** | From the cockpit. Your car body is hidden so nothing blocks the view |

## Cockpit view
Press **I** (phone: **VIEW**) to fly from inside your car. Press it again, or **C**, to go back outside. The game
remembers your choice for the next race. It works in RACE, TIME ATTACK and FREE ROAM.

Every car has its own cockpit, and its screens show your real numbers:

| Screen | Shows |
|---|---|
| Speed | Speed in km/h or mph |
| BOOST | Boost left, in % and as a bar |
| PULSE | **READY**, the blaster's heat once it passes 60%, or **OVERHEAT** while it cools down |
| Lap and time | **LAP 1/2** and the lap time (TIME ATTACK: the lap number; FREE ROAM: **FREE ROAM** and your time online) |

The yoke (ROADSTER), side grips (VIPER) or wheel (TITAN) turns as you steer. The weapons panel, map, coins and
markers stay on screen as usual. Holding the right mouse button to look around shows the outside view until you let go.

![ROADSTER cockpit](../media/v0.2/cockpit-roadster.jpg)
![VIPER cockpit](../media/v0.2/cockpit-viper.jpg)
![TITAN cockpit](../media/v0.2/cockpit-titan.jpg)

**Look around:** hold the **right mouse button** and move the mouse to swing the camera around your car. The car
keeps flying its course while you look. Let go and the view swings back behind the car.

The camera also widens with speed, shakes on impacts and pulls back when you are wrecked.

## Collisions
Buildings, the deck, rings, signs and other props are solid. Hitting them bounces you off and costs speed (a hard hit
cuts your speed by 30%). Cars bump into each other too. Collisions don't damage your shield; only weapons do.

## Reset and warnings
| Message | Meaning | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **CHECKPOINT 3/8 · 0:41.20** | You passed a ring | Boost +30%, and this ring becomes your reset point |
| **MISSED GATE** | You crossed the start gate without passing all 8 rings | The lap doesn't count; go back for the ring you missed |
| **WRONG WAY** | You've been flying backwards along the track for over a second | Turn around |
| **SIGNAL LOST** | You dropped below the clouds (under 34 m) or flew more than 650 m from the track | After about a second you're put back at your last checkpoint |
| **FINAL LAP** / **LAP 2** | A new lap started | With **★ BEST LAP** if it was your fastest |

Press **R** at any time to reset yourself to your last checkpoint, flying forward at a moderate speed.
In FREE ROAM, **R** takes you back to your spawn point instead (see [Free Roam](06-free-roam.md#respawning)).

## Pause
**Esc** (phone: **II**, gamepad: **Start**) pauses the game. The pause screen shows your car up close:
**drag** to turn it and **scroll** to zoom. Buttons: **RESUME**, **RESTART**, **MAIN MENU**.

![Pause screen](../media/v0.1/pause.jpg)

In FREE ROAM the world doesn't stop: other players keep flying while you're on the pause screen. Your car hangs where
you left it, and other players can still hit and wreck it (since 0.2.8): pause somewhere quiet.
