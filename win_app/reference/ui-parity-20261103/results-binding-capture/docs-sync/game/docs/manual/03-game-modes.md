## The roads
There is one map, **SKYLINE CITY**, and **nine roads** through it. Every road is a closed loop in the sky that starts
and ends on the same floating **deck** under the **start gate**. Each road is longer and harder than the one before:
more corners, tighter ones, bigger climbs and dives, more gates, and rivals that are a little faster (2% a road) and
aim a little better.

| Road | Name | Lap | Gates | Unlocks by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SKYLINE LOOP | 5.64 km | 8 | open from the start |
| 2 | VIOLET RUN | 6.23 km | 9 | winning road 1 |
| 3 | GLASS CANYON | 6.84 km | 10 | winning road 2 |
| 4 | HALO DRIFT | 7.42 km | 11 | winning road 3 |
| 5 | NEON SPIRAL | 8.02 km | 13 | winning road 4 |
| 6 | STORM LINE | 8.61 km | 14 | winning road 5 |
| 7 | RAZOR CROWN | 9.21 km | 16 | winning road 6 |
| 8 | VOID SPRINT | 9.81 km | 17 | winning road 7 |
| 9 | APEX OVERDRIVE | 10.40 km | 19 | winning road 8 |

- **Choosing a road:** **RACE** and **TIME ATTACK** open the road list first (**Enter** on the menu does too). Click a
  road to start. The bars show how hard it is; the right side shows your best lap there.
- **Unlocking:** road 1 is open. Win a road in **1st place in RACE** to open the next one. The results screen then
  shows *🔓 ROAD 2 · VIOLET RUN UNLOCKED* and a **NEXT ROAD ›** button. Locked roads show *WIN ROAD n IN 1ST*.
  Signed in, your progress is saved to your account and follows you to other devices.
- The game remembers the road you picked last; the menu shows it (*ROAD 1 · SKYLINE LOOP · 8 GATES · 5.64 KM*).
- **Road 1, SKYLINE LOOP:** a long launch climb, a high sweeping turn, a dive down to the clouds, another climb, a dive
  into a hairpin, and the run home over the deck, with **8 checkpoint rings** between 80 and 250 m altitude.
- Roads 2-9 fly between about 90 and 320 m and reach further across the city. Fly through every gate in order.
- A glowing **guidance line** shows the racing line; beacons, barriers and signs mark the turns.
- The **minimap** (top right) shows the loop, the checkpoints and the cars.

## RACE
*Two laps against 10 AI rivals.* The main mode.

![Race countdown](../media/v0.1/race-countdown.jpg)

1. You line up in the middle of a 12-slot grid on the deck. The countdown runs **3, 2, 1, GO!**
2. Fly through the road's rings in order, then through the start gate to complete a lap. Each ring tops up your boost by 30%.
3. **Coins** float in short zig-zag strings between the rings. The first car to touch a coin takes it
   ([Progress](07-progress.md#coins)).
4. Rivals race each other too, shoot each other and, now and then, you. Fight back with your
   [weapons](05-combat.md).
5. After 2 laps: *1st / 2nd / … PLACE*, then the results screen.

![Race in progress](../media/v0.1/race-hud.jpg)

The 10 rivals are **VOLT, RAZOR, NOVA, HEX, ZERO, ONYX, KAIRO, VEX, LUX** and **AXIOM**. Each has its own car color
and style. Since 0.2.15 they fly **120 m/s** (432 km/h, 268 mph) on road 1 and 2% faster on each road after it, up to
141 m/s (506 km/h, 315 mph) on road 9, more on nitro; they slow down for the bends like you have to. The best of them
boost more often. They fight each other and you with your own weapons: blaster bolts (about one every 0.6 seconds),
seekers, the spin dodge and the EMP ([Combat](05-combat.md#how-rivals-fight-race)). A rival far behind you gets a little faster to catch up; a rival ahead of you is never slowed down.

### Results
![Results screen](../media/v0.1/race-results.jpg)

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Place | Your finishing place and takedowns, e.g. *3RD OF 11 · 2 TAKEDOWNS* |
| Time | Your total race time |
| Laps | Each lap time; the best one is highlighted |
| Standings | Everyone's time. Rivals who haven't finished get an estimate (marked **≈**) |
| **COIN RACE** | Who collected most coins (♛ = winner), your coin-race place and any podium bonus |
| Online | What was saved: *SAVED +23 · WALLET 673 · WORLD RANK #4*, and a new best 2-lap time if you set one |

Buttons: **RACE AGAIN** and **MAIN MENU**. If you're not signed in, a button lets you sign in and still save this race.

## TIME ATTACK
*Solo laps against the clock.* No rivals and no coins. Laps continue until you quit, so you can keep improving.
Your **BEST LAP** is saved on this device and shown on the menu and in the lap panel.

![Time attack](../media/v0.1/time-attack.jpg)

## FREE ROAM
*The online city.* Fly anywhere with other signed-in players. There are no laps, gates or coins. Fly, chat and fight;
wrecking another player counts as a kill for the **FREE ROAM KILLS** ranking. You need a Google sign-in and a
nickname. The menu button shows how many people are flying right now.

Everything about it is in [Free Roam online](06-free-roam.md).
