# Glossary

[Documentation home](README.md)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Boost / nitro** | Extra speed (up to 196 m/s = 706 km/h) that drains the BOOST bar. Shift or BOOST |
| **Bolt** | One shot of the PULSE blaster |
| **Car showcase** | The camera around your car on the main menu (swaying in front of it, with an LED screen behind), the pause screen and the shop (slowly circling) |
| **Checkpoint** | One of the 8 rings on the SKYLINE LOOP; pass them in order |
| **Cockpit view** | Flying from inside your car with its dashboard (I, or VIEW on phones) |
| **CRIMSON FLAME** | The Killer aura from 3 kills: crimson underglow, side flames, pod fire wreaths, ember trail |
| **VOID CROWN** | The Killer aura from 10 kills: a crystal crown above the roof, circling shards, violet underglow, side flames, pod wreaths, ember trail |
| **Coin race** | The side contest in RACE: who collects the most coins; podium bonus 15/8/4 |
| **EMP** | Area blast: 25 damage to everyone within 95 m, 11 s cooldown |
| **FREE ROAM** | Online mode: fly the open city with other players and fight them |
| **Free look** | Holding the right mouse button to look around without steering |
| **Heat** | PULSE blaster temperature; at 100 % it overheats and stops firing until cool |
| **Hub** | The FREE ROAM start platform (24 slots) where you spawn and respawn |
| **Kill** | In FREE ROAM, wrecking another player; saved to your lifetime kills |
| **Killer** | KILLER STATUS: a player with 3 FREE ROAM player kills. Wears the CRIMSON FLAME (the VOID CROWN from 10 kills), can't use R, until /pkclear |
| **Lock-on** | Keeping a target near the aim for 0.5 s so a SEEKER homes in |
| **Mock mode** | Developer mode (`?mock` + `LIVE_MOCK=1`) with fake sign-in and a local FREE ROAM server |
| **Protocol** | Version number of the FREE ROAM messages (`LIVE_PROTOCOL`, 4 in v0.1, 6 in v0.2.1, 7 in v0.2.4); client and server must match |
| **PULSE** | The rapid-fire blaster (left mouse / FIRE) |
| **Relay** | The FREE ROAM server (`server/live.js`) that passes positions, shots, hits and chat between players |
| **Race ticket** | A server note that your RACE or TIME ATTACK started; finishing needs it to save coins and times |
| **Road** | One of the nine race courses through the city (`src/roads.js`), each with its own gates, best lap and TIME ranking; the next one unlocks when you win the current one in 1st place |
| **Rival** | A computer-driven car in RACE (10 per race) |
| **Rubber band** | AI speed adjustment that keeps rivals close to the player |
| **Session kills** | Kills in the current FREE ROAM connection, shown in the roster; they reset when you reconnect (lifetime kills don't) |
| **Slot** | One of the 24 spawn places on the hub; a player holds one while connected |
| **/pkclear** | FREE ROAM chat command: clears your KILLER STATUS |
| **SEEKER** | Homing missile: 48 damage, 3 charges, one recharges every 4.5 s |
| **Shop** | Main-menu screen to buy cars (VIPER, TITAN) with wallet coins; each car has its own stats (v0.2.17) |
| **Shield** | Your health (100). At 0 you are wrecked |
| **Shooter-decided hit** | FREE ROAM rule: the shooter's game decides if a shot hit, the victim's game applies the damage |
| **SKYLINE LOOP** | The race track: a 2-lap circuit through the city |
| **Snapshot** | One batch of all players' positions sent by the relay (about 15 per second) |
| **SPIN** | Barrel-roll dodge: a quick sideways hop with a speed surge, 6 s cooldown |
| **Takedown** | Wrecking a rival in RACE |
| **Time Attack** | Solo mode: fly laps against the clock, no rivals |
| **Wallet** | Your spendable coins (`coins`), spent in the shop; **total coins** (`totalCoins`) is the lifetime figure used for ranking and never goes down |
| **Wrecked** | Destroyed: shield at 0. In FREE ROAM you respawn at the hub after 3 s, with 3 s of safety |
