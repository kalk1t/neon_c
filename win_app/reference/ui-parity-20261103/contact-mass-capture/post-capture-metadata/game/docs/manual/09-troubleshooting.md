# 9. Troubleshooting and FAQ

## The game is slow or choppy
1. Open **SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS** on the main menu. If the GPU line says **Software renderer: hardware acceleration
   looks OFF**, turn on hardware acceleration in your browser (Chrome/Edge: Settings → System → *Use graphics
   acceleration when available*) and restart the browser. This is the most common cause.
2. Set **GRAPHICS** to **LOW · FASTER**.
3. Close other heavy tabs and programs. On a laptop, plug it in (battery saving slows the graphics chip).
4. In FREE ROAM, the **FPS** number shows the effect of each change right away.

## Error screens
| Screen | What to do |
|---|---|
| **3D GRAPHICS NOT AVAILABLE** | Your browser can't run WebGL 2. Update it (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and turn on hardware acceleration |
| **GRAPHICS RESET** | Your graphics driver restarted. Reload the page. Choosing LOW graphics can help |
| **THE GAME COULD NOT START** | Reload the page. If it keeps happening, email help@roadstersrace.com with the message shown |
| **TURN YOUR PHONE** | Hold the phone sideways |

## Controls
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The mouse doesn't steer | Click the game to capture the mouse. Some browsers refuse for about a second after you press Esc; click again |
| The game pauses by itself | Pressing Esc or switching windows releases the mouse, which pauses the game. Click **RESUME** |
| The car keeps climbing or diving (phone) | Tap **◎ TILT** while holding the phone the way you like |
| Tilt steering does nothing (phone) | On iPhone, allow motion access when asked. Otherwise drag on the left half of the screen to steer |
| Up and down feel reversed | Toggle **INVERT Y** |
| The gamepad does nothing | Press any button on it once while the game is open |

## Online
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **SIGN IN TO DRIVE ONLINE** in FREE ROAM | Sign in again with **SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE** |
| The Google pop-up doesn't open | Allow pop-ups for roadstersrace.com |
| *RACES TOO CLOSE TOGETHER · SAVING IN N S* | Races can be saved once a minute. Wait on the results screen: it saves by itself when the countdown ends |
| *NOT SAVED YET* with **TRY AGAIN** | The connection dropped. Click TRY AGAIN when you're back online |
| **GAME UPDATED · RELOADING…** | A new version is out. The page reloads itself once; nothing is lost |
| **SERVER UPDATING · TRY AGAIN SOON** | The server is restarting. Wait a minute, then join FREE ROAM again |
| FREE ROAM shows **RECONNECTING…** | The connection dropped; the game retries by itself (up to every 15 s) |
| My kill isn't in my profile yet | Kills are saved at most once a minute. If you close the tab before that, they're saved the next time you open the game on the same device and browser |
| High ping (red **MS**) | Use a wired connection or move closer to the Wi-Fi router; close downloads and streams |

## FAQ
**Do I need an account?** Only for FREE ROAM, the World Ranking and saving coins. Racing and Time Attack work without one.

**Is it free?** Yes.

**What can I buy with coins?** New cars in the **SHOP**: VIPER (2,500) and TITAN (7,500). Each flies and fights differently (since 0.2.17). See [Progress](07-progress.md#shop).

**My race says NOT SAVED.** Coins are saved only for a RACE you were signed in for when it started, at most once
a minute. The message says which rule stopped it ([Progress](07-progress.md#wallet)).

**Can I change my nickname?** Yes: **EDIT** in the account panel. Your old nickname becomes free for others.

**Why did my kills reset?** The player list shows kills **this session**, which start at 0 each time you join. Your
lifetime total is on the menu and in your account panel.

**Does it work offline?** The game needs the internet to load. Without online services, races still work, but
nothing is saved.

**How do I report a bug or suggest something?** Email **help@roadstersrace.com**. Include what you were doing, your
device and browser, and a screenshot if you can.
