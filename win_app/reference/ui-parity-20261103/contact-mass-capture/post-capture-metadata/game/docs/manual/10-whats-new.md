# 10. What's new in 0.2

🔄 **Changing** · [Manual contents](README.md) · Previous: [Troubleshooting](09-troubleshooting.md)

## 0.2.17
- **Each car is different now.** The VIPER is the fastest and most agile but fragile; the TITAN is slower but tough,
  hardly pushed around, with 4 seekers and a bigger EMP; the ROADSTER is the all-rounder. The shop shows each car's
  stats. They count in RACE and in FREE ROAM.

## 0.2.16
- **Rivals fight like you.** Besides blaster bolts they fire seekers (watch for **SEEKER INCOMING**), spin away from
  yours and set off EMPs when cars crowd them. They now see targets in a wider angle in front of them.

## 0.2.15
- **Faster rivals.** They fly 120 m/s (432 km/h) on road 1 and 2% faster on every road after it, brake for the bends,
  and don't slow down any more when they're ahead of you.
- **Rivals shoot your blaster's bolts**: long range, 21 damage, about one every 0.6 seconds.
- **Your car is faster:** about 486 km/h, 706 km/h on nitro.
- **Settings: SPEED** shows km/h or mph.

## 0.2.14
- **A stronger blaster.** Each bolt now does 21 damage (5 wreck a car), flies about 1.1 km and you can fire about 22
  before it overheats. Seekers lock on from up to 2 km away.
- **Bolts in your car's colour**, bigger and brighter, fired from your car's front bumper.
- The car leans further into a sideways slide.

## 0.2.13
- **Slide sideways with A / D.** With the mouse, A and D now move the car to the side instead of turning it (the
  mouse does the turning): dodge shots or line up a gate while your aim stays on target. The arrows still turn.
- **No more shaking when you turn a stopped car.**

## 0.2.12
- **One aim ring.** With the mouse, the view now follows your aim: the ring stays in the middle of the screen, your car
  flies to it and your shots go there. A small dot shows where the nose points while the car catches up.
- **Flying backwards with the mouse works:** the car no longer spins round.
- **Right-click look-around no longer gets stuck.**

## 0.2.11
- Behind the scenes: website security preparations. Nothing changes in the game.

## 0.2.10
- **Fairer FREE ROAM fights.** The server now checks every hit: only real shots, fired from where the shooter is and
  able to reach you, count. Nothing changes for fair players.

## 0.2.9
- **Safer FREE ROAM chat.** Rude words are hidden with stars. Type **/mute NAME** to hide someone's messages on your
  device (**/unmute NAME** to undo, **/muted** to list), **/report NAME** to send their recent messages to us, and
  **/help** for all commands. Players who keep abusing the chat can be banned from FREE ROAM.

## 0.2.8
- **No hiding in the pause menu in FREE ROAM.** The city doesn't stop when you pause: other players can still hit and wreck your car.

## 0.2.7
- Behind the scenes: extra protection for the website and the FREE ROAM server. Nothing changes in the game.

## 0.2.6
- Behind the scenes: the game's code is reorganised so it is easier to improve. Nothing changes in the game.

## 0.2.5
- Security fix behind the scenes; nothing changes in the game.

## 0.2.4
- **Your shop car in FREE ROAM.** Other players now see the VIPER or TITAN you drive (it used to show as a ROADSTER
  for everyone).
- **Fair rankings.** Kills and Killer status are now saved by the server, and race results are checked harder: times
  faster than a road allows don't count, and a day's coin earnings are capped at 2,000 (far above what real races
  make), so nobody can cheat their way up the rankings.
- **Faster loading for returning players.** The 3D engine is downloaded once and reused across updates.
- `/pkclear` now needs a connection to FREE ROAM (the server saves it).

## 0.2.3
- **Tougher rivals.** All ten rivals now fly as fast as your car (they used to be up to 8% slower), and they hit
  harder and fire more often: **1.5×** on every road. A rival shot now takes 30 of your 100 hp. Later roads are still
  harder than earlier ones.
- **TRACK FLYOVER has been removed** from the main menu. The menu now goes straight to the racing: RACE, TIME
  ATTACK, FREE ROAM, the ranking and the shop.
- Behind the scenes: safer updates (new versions are tested before they go live and can be rolled back in seconds)
  and extra protection against bots (App Check).

## 0.2.1
- **VOID CROWN.** Reach **10 kills** as a Killer and your CRIMSON FLAME becomes the **VOID CROWN**: a crystal crown
  above your car, crystal shards circling it, and violet flames. Everyone sees it. See
  [Free Roam](06-free-roam.md#killer-status).
- **Race results save reliably.** Pressing RACE AGAIN right after a race no longer loses it. Two races finished
  within a minute: the second one counts down on the results screen and saves by itself. (The gap was 2 minutes.)

## 0.2
Version 0.2 is live on roadstersrace.com since 1 November 2026. Highlights:

- **Nine roads.** The city now has nine roads, from the 5.64 km SKYLINE LOOP to the 10.40 km APEX OVERDRIVE, each
  longer and harder than the last. Win a road in **1st place** to unlock the next. RACE and TIME ATTACK start by
  choosing a road. See [the roads](03-game-modes.md#the-roads).
- **World ranking: COIN · TIME · KILLS.** TIME now has its own ranking for every road. See
  [Progress](07-progress.md#world-ranking).
- **New menu view**: your car hovers in front of a giant LED screen in the city.
- **The SHOP.** Spend your wallet on two new cars: **VIPER** (2,500 coins) and **TITAN** (7,500 coins). Cars change
  the looks only; everyone has the same speed and weapons. Your lifetime coins and coin rank never go down when you
  spend. See [Progress](07-progress.md#shop).
- **Cockpit view.** Press **I** (phone: **VIEW**) to fly from inside your car. Each car has its own dashboard with live
  speed, boost, blaster and lap time. See [Flying](04-flying.md#cockpit-view).
- **Safer coins.** The game's server now checks every race before saving coins and does every purchase itself, so
  nobody can fake coins into the ranking. Sign in **before** you race: a race started signed out can't be saved.
- **KILLER STATUS.** Wreck 3 players in FREE ROAM and your car bursts into the **CRIMSON FLAME**, on every car, in
  every mode, for everyone to see. R respawn is locked until you type **/pkclear** in the chat. See
  [Free Roam](06-free-roam.md#killer-status).
- **FREE ROAM respawn**: after being wrecked you're down for 3 seconds, with a countdown.
- **New coins on the track**: the same gold wheel coin as in the shop.
- **Version number** on the main menu (**v0.2 · ONLINE**).

The full list, including technical changes, is in the [changelog](../changelog.md).
