# 8. Settings

✅ **Done** · [Manual contents](README.md) · Previous: [Progress](07-progress.md) · Next: [Troubleshooting](09-troubleshooting.md)

All settings are on the main menu and are remembered on this device (in the browser's storage).

| Setting | Values | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MOUSE SENS** | 0.3 – 2.5 | 1.0 | How far the aim dot moves per mouse movement. Computers only |
| **INVERT Y** | on / off | off | Mouse up = dive, and swaps climb/dive for phone tilt |
| **VOLUME** | 0 – 100% | 70% | Plays a coin sound so you can hear the level |
| **GRAPHICS** | HIGH / LOW · FASTER | HIGH (phones: LOW) | See below |
| **CAR COLOR** | 11 paint jobs | ORIGINAL | See below |
| Mute | **M** key | – | Toggles all sound |

## Graphics
| | HIGH | LOW · FASTER |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | Up to 1.5× your screen's pixels | 0.75× |
| Edge smoothing (anti-aliasing) | 4× | Off |
| Speed blur | On | Off |

If you've never chosen, the game measures your frame rate for the first few seconds on the main menu and switches to
**LOW** by itself if it's below 40 FPS. Your own choice always wins.

## Car color
Eleven paint jobs: **ORIGINAL** (dark purple with magenta) and the colors of the 10 rival teams: **VOLT, RAZOR, NOVA,
HEX, ZERO, ONYX, KAIRO, VEX, LUX, AXIOM**. Click a swatch; the car on the menu repaints immediately.
In FREE ROAM, other players see your car in your chosen color, and your shots glow in its accent color.

## Privacy
- **Analytics:** a banner asks once whether the game may send anonymous play statistics (Google Analytics).
  Nothing is sent unless you click **ALLOW**. Change your answer with **ANALYTICS SETTINGS** at the bottom right of the menu.
- **Privacy** and **Terms** links are at the bottom right of the menu.
- Contact: **help@roadstersrace.com** (also on the pause screen).
