ROADSTERS RACE - SOURCE BUILD / OFFLINE SKYLINE PROTOTYPE

Current source has TWO-LAP TRIAL and endless TIME ATTACK on Skyline Loop,
countdown, ordered checkpoints, lap history, offline results, wrong-way and
timed signal-lost recovery. It does not include rivals, combat, rewards,
progression, accounts, online play or full browser visuals.

Current compiled native build:
build/road-picker-native/bin/RoadstersRace.exe
It has passed 8/8 headless tests and a scoped native functional UI check.
Complete-race/results visuals, broader graphics and performance remain open.
Run-RoadstersRace.cmd still points to the older dist/first-flight package.
No launcher or existing package was replaced by this continuation.

Enter opens the trial road picker; TIME ATTACK has a separate menu button.
Use Up/Down and Enter in the picker, or click a row; Esc/Back returns to menu.
Only road 1 is open in this clean offline prototype. No saved browser/account
progress is imported, and trial finishes grant no road unlocks or rewards.
Per-road SESSION BEST times last only for this application run.
Start inputs require a focused release frame. Countdown ignores flight input.
W thrust / S brake-reverse; mouse aim; A/D steer; E/Q climb-dive;
Shift boost; F spin; R reset to safe transform; C near/far chase camera;
P or Esc pause; F11 fullscreen. Physical gamepad parity remains unverified.
Each new lap requires every checkpoint and then the start/finish gantry.
Two-lap results are local trial times; they award or upload nothing.

Conservative rendering remains: deferred 3D load, 30 FPS, bounded internal
target at most 960x600, MSAA off and glow off. Focus loss pauses flight.
A slow-frame pause cannot interrupt a blocked driver call or guarantee that
the earlier reported whole-PC freeze cannot recur. Its cause is unresolved.

Prior menu-only and interrupted flight evidence refer to an earlier executable.
