rr_ai_current_corner_limit scans ahead to speed^2/(2*45)+40m and takes min(sqrt(cornerSpeed^2+2*45*distance)). The native scan stops at one full lap: a repeated sample has the same cornerSpeed at a greater distance, so it cannot lower the minimum. This is faithful bounded work rather than distance/detail omission; 18 actual-source overspeed multi-lap comparisons check it. Repeated-distance addition retains source order.

Relative to corner limit, throttle stops above -4m/s, brakes above 6m/s or heading error 0.55rad, and nitro requires at least 25m/s headroom plus the source heading/boost/driver tests. Steering/climb clamp exactly as source; rivals receive no strafe. Current road top and ranking-speed APIs implement 120*1.02^(id-1), normalized by 135, times 1+clamp(gap*0.05,0,0.07).
